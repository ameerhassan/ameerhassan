Privacy Policy

Quran Al-Kareem — Audio, Tafsir & Translation
Publisher: Hidayah Apps
Effective date: 2026-11-12

Hidayah Apps built the Quran Al-Kareem app as a free service. This page explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the app.

Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, the app may process:

  • App diagnostics — crash reports and performance signals via Firebase Crashlytics
  • Analytics — anonymous usage events via Firebase Analytics (for example screen views and feature use)
  • Approximate or precise location — only if you enable prayer times / Qibla features that need device location (Android phones; not used on Android TV)
  • On-device preferences — bookmarks, reading progress, language, and audio settings stored locally on your device

We do not require an account to use the core Quran reading features.

Third-party services

We use Google Firebase / Google Play services. Their policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy

How we use information

  • To provide Quran reading, audio, translations, and prayer tools
  • To improve stability and understand which features are used
  • To enforce critical app updates when configured via Remote Config

Data retention & security

Local reading data stays on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Crash and analytics data are processed by Google Firebase according to their retention policies. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising profiles. Parents who believe a child provided personal information can contact us to request deletion where applicable.

Your choices

  • Deny location permission — prayer/Qibla GPS features will be limited; reading still works
  • Disable notifications in system settings — reminders / adhan alerts will stop
  • Uninstall the app to remove local data from the device

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: contact Hidayah Apps through the Google Play listing for Quran Al-Kareem (Play Store).
